The Silicon Shield: Malaysia's E&E Trade Performance

Visualising the explosive growth, geographic dominance, and global network of Malaysia's Electrical, Electronics, and Machinery exports.

1. The Macro Trend: Two Decades of Export Evolution

Driven by the global surge in digital transformation and the post-pandemic semiconductor super-cycle, Malaysia's E&E exports have shattered historical records. While the early 2000s experienced steady, cyclical patterns, the trajectory from 2021 onwards reveals an unprecedented exponential boom.

2. State-level Dominance: The Silicon Valleys of Malaysia (2025)

Geographically, the wealth generated by E&E and machinery exports is heavily concentrated. Penang and Selangor stand as absolute titans in the supply chain, accounting for the lion's share of the national export volume.
