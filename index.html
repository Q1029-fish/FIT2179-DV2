The Silicon Shield: Malaysia's Tech Export Engine

Visualising the explosive growth, geographic concentration, and global network of Malaysia's Electrical, Electronics (E&E), and Machinery exports.

Act I: The Phenomenon & The Core

1. The Macro Trend: Two Decades of Export Evolution

What is driving Malaysia's current economic narrative? Driven by global digital transformation and the post-pandemic semiconductor super-cycle, E&E exports have shattered historical records. Notice the unprecedented exponential boom post-2021. (Brush the timeline below to zoom in on specific eras).

2. Export Structure

While the dataset covers "Machinery and Transport", the overwhelming engine of this growth is raw Electronic & Electrical components.

3. Category Trends

Stripping away the noise: Integrated Circuits (Microchips) are the uncontested pioneers, leaving traditional telecommunications and computing parts far behind.

Act II: The Geography of Power

4. Geographic Dominance: Malaysia's Silicon Valleys

Where is this wealth generated? The tech manufacturing sector is heavily polarized. Penang and Selangor stand as absolute titans, functioning as the central hubs for semiconductor fabrication and high-end machinery assembly.

5. The Rise of the Titans: State-level Trajectories

Separating the historical growth trajectories of Malaysia's top 4 tech hubs reveals a stark reality: Penang's post-2020 acceleration has completely outpaced its peers, cementing its status as the 'Silicon Valley of the East'.

Act III: The Global Dependency

6. Global Reach: Where Do Our Tech Products Go?

Malaysia does not consume these chips; it feeds the world. This choropleth map visualizes the vast international footprint of local tech manufacturing, highlighting our critical role in the global supply chain.

7. Top Global Buyers

Singapore (as a transshipment hub), the US, and China form the golden triangle of demand, consistently absorbing the majority of the output.

8. Partner Market Share

A normalized view showing how the balance of power among our top trading partners fluctuates over time, reflecting global trade tensions and shifts.

Act IV: Rhythms & Exploration

9. Seasonal Surges: When Does Export Peak?

Manufacturing output is cyclical. Notice the consistent darkening of colors towards the third and fourth quarters (Q3/Q4) as factories rush to meet global holiday tech demands.
