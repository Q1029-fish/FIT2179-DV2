The Silicon Crossroads

Visualising Malaysia's critical role in the global supply chain of Electrical & Electronic (E&E) and Machinery trade.

Act I: The Core Engine

1. The Macro Trend: Unprecedented Export Boom

Driven by global digital transformation, Malaysia's tech exports have shattered records. Notice the exponential boom post-2021. (Brush the timeline below to zoom).

2. Export Structure

This donut chart clarifies the fundamental baseline: Within the vast "Machinery and Transport" sector, the absolute engine is raw Electronic & Electrical components.

3. The Assembly Line Reality

A massive export engine requires massive fuel. Looking solely at the Tech & Machinery sector (SITC 7), we see imports mirroring exports almost perfectly. This reveals our true role: a global testing hub.

Act II: The Global Supply Chain

4. Import Origins

This interactive map reveals our heavy reliance on economies for raw wafers and specialized machinery. Showing the global trade network.

5. Export Destinations

After assembly, products are funneled into consumer markets. Compare with imports to see Malaysia's true function: a global transit hub for tech components.

Act III: Power Dynamics & Trade Wars

6. Top Global Buyers

A sorted view of the golden triangle of demand. Singapore acts as a massive transshipment hub, immediately followed by the superpowers: the US and China.

7. Partner Market Share

A normalized perspective showing how the balance of power among our top 3 trading partners fluctuates over time amid global geopolitical tensions.

8. The Trade Balance: Imports vs. Exports

Are we in a trade surplus or deficit with specific partners? Countries plotted higher on the Y-axis represent strong export destinations, while bubble size indicates total trade volume.

Act IV: Rhythms & Exploration

9. Seasonal Surges: When Does Export Peak?

Tech manufacturing is highly cyclical. Notice the consistent darkening of colors towards the third and fourth quarters (Q3/Q4) as factories rush to meet global holiday demands.
