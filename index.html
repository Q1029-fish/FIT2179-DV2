4. What Exactly Are We Exporting?

While E&E is a broad category, the true engine of Malaysia's export economy is raw semiconductor components, specifically Integrated Circuits (Chips).

5. The Trade Balance: Imports vs. Exports

A healthy tech ecosystem requires both buying and selling. This bubble chart plots our major partners. Countries plotted higher on the Y-axis represent strong export destinations, while the size of the bubble indicates the total trade volume.

6. Growth Pillars: Product Category Trends

Tracking the growth of different sub-sectors over time reveals that Integrated Circuits remain the uncontested pioneer driving Malaysia's export boom, outstripping other electronic components significantly.

7. Shifting Alliances: Top Buyers' Market Share

By normalizing the export volumes to 100%, we can analyze the relative market share of our top 3 trading partners. This reveals subtle shifts in supply chain reliance over the past five years.

8. Seasonal Surges: When Does Export Peak?

Is there a seasonal pattern to the semiconductor trade? This heatmap visualizes export intensity by month and year. Notice the consistent darkening of colors towards the end of each year, likely driven by global consumer electronics manufacturing cycles ahead of holiday seasons.

9. The Rise of the Titans: Top 4 States Trajectory

Instead of cluttering a single chart, these "small multiples" separate the growth trajectories of Malaysia's top 4 tech hubs. While Penang and Johor show aggressive exponential growth, Kedah is emerging as a steady new player.
