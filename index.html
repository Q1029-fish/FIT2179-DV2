Malaysia's Position in Asia's Technology Supply Chain

An analysis of machinery and electronics trade (2006–2025).

Chapter 1 Growth of Malaysia's Technology Trade

1. Technology Exports Have Expanded Rapidly Since 2006

Malaysia's SITC 7 exports have grown substantially over the past two decades, accelerating after 2020 as demand for semiconductors, electronics, and digital infrastructure increased across Asia.

2. Electrical and Electronic Products Dominate Export Composition

Although SITC 7 includes various machinery categories, electrical and electronic products account for the largest share of Malaysia's technology exports, highlighting the country's specialization in electronics manufacturing.

3. Imports Grow Alongside Exports

Import and export values move closely together over time. This suggests that Malaysia relies heavily on imported components and equipment before re-exporting higher-value products to regional markets.

Chapter 2 Asia-Wide Trade Connections

4. Major Sources of Technology Imports in Asia (2025)

Malaysia imports large volumes of components, wafers, and manufacturing equipment from several Asian economies, particularly China, Singapore, Taiwan, Japan, and South Korea.

5. Major Export Destinations in Asia (2025)

Technology products manufactured or assembled in Malaysia are primarily exported to neighboring Asian markets, with Singapore and China serving as major destinations.

Chapter 3 Trade Relationships and Market Dependence

6. Top Asian Export Markets in 2025

Singapore and China remain Malaysia's largest technology export markets, accounting for a substantial share of regional trade demand.

7. Changing Market Shares of Key Trading Partners

Although Singapore remains the largest destination, the relative importance of major trading partners has shifted over time, reflecting changing regional economic conditions.

8. Trade Surplus and Deficit with Major Asian Partners

Malaysia records trade surpluses with some economies and deficits with others. These differences reveal Malaysia's position within the regional production network and highlight varying levels of dependence on imported technology inputs.

Chapter 4 Seasonal Patterns and Product Insights

9. Seasonal Export Patterns Across Years

Export activity tends to strengthen during the second half of the year, reflecting production cycles and increased demand before year-end retail and manufacturing peaks.
