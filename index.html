The Silicon Shield: Malaysia's Tech Export Engine

Visualising the explosive growth, geographic dominance, and global network of Malaysia's Electrical, Electronics (E&E) and Machinery exports.

1. The Macro Trend: Two Decades of Export Evolution

Driven by global digital transformation and the semiconductor super-cycle, Malaysia's E&E and machinery exports have shattered records. The trajectory post-2021 reveals an unprecedented exponential boom. (Brush the timeline below to zoom).

2. Geographic Dominance: Malaysia's Silicon Valleys

The wealth generated by tech exports is heavily concentrated. Penang and Selangor stand as absolute titans, functioning as the central hubs for semiconductor fabrication and machinery assembly.

3. Top Global Buyers

Singapore, the US, and China form the golden triangle of demand, consistently absorbing the majority of Malaysia's E&E output.

4. Export Structure

While the "Machinery" category is broad, the true engine is raw Electronic & Electrical components, primarily microchips.

5. Global Reach: Where Do Our Tech Products Go?

Beyond the top 3 buyers, Malaysia's E&E and machinery exports penetrate markets globally. This choropleth map visualizes the vast international footprint of local tech manufacturing.

6. Category Trends

This multi-line chart confirms that Integrated Circuits (Semiconductors) remain the uncontested pioneer driving the recent export surge.

7. Partner Market Share

A normalized view showing how the balance of power among our top trading partners fluctuates in the tech sector.

8. Seasonal Surges: When Does Export Peak?

Manufacturing output isn't flat. Notice the consistent darkening of colors towards the third and fourth quarters as factories rush to meet global holiday tech demands.

9. The Rise of the Titans: State-level Trajectories

Separating the growth trajectories of Malaysia's top 4 tech hubs reveals that Penang's post-2020 acceleration completely outpaces other regions.
