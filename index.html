The Silicon Crossroads

Visualising Malaysia's critical role in the global supply chain of Electrical & Electronic (E&E) and Machinery trade.

Act I: The Core Engine

1. The Macro Trend: Unprecedented Export Boom

Driven by global digital transformation, Malaysia's tech exports have shattered records. Notice the steady growth turning into an exponential boom post-2021.

2. Export Structure

This donut chart clarifies the fundamental baseline: Within the vast export sector, the absolute engine is raw Electronic & Electrical components.

3. The Assembly Line Reality

A massive export engine requires massive fuel. The parallel growth of imports and exports validates Malaysia's position as a highly integrated processing node.

Act II: The Asian Semiconductor Corridor

4. Import Origins

This map reveals our heavy regional reliance on Asian economies for raw wafers and specialized machinery, forming the foundation of our supply chain.

5. Export Destinations

The parity between import and export origins is an indicator of Malaysia's deep-rooted integration into the circular Asian semiconductor value chain.

Act III: Power Dynamics & Trade Wars

6. Top Global Buyers (Volume & Intensity)

A sorted view of the golden triangle of demand. The length of the bar represents export volume, while the color intensity reveals their dependence (Intensity Index) on our supply.

7. Core Partner Growth Trajectory

Instead of static market share, this trend line reveals the sheer absolute volume surge among our top 3 partners, highlighting an expanding global reliance post-2021.

8. The Trade Balance: Imports vs. Exports

Are we in a trade surplus or deficit with specific partners? Countries plotted higher on the Y-axis represent strong export destinations.

Act IV: Rhythms & Exploration

9. Seasonal Surges: When Does Export Peak?

Tech manufacturing is highly cyclical. Notice the consistent darkening of colors towards the third and fourth quarters (Q3/Q4).
